## Who I'm writing for

Bangalore founders, builders, designers, operators and investors, and the people
who go to meetups. Local: they know Koramangala, HSR, Indiranagar, ORR traffic,
the meetup circuit and the local startup names. Don't explain them.

## What I sound like here

- A bit more intellectual than my other accounts. Observations and takes, not
  hype.
- Lowercase sentence starts are fine. Names stay capitalised.
- Short. One idea per post.
- Emoji: almost never. Hashtags: only an event's own tag.
- Sharp about patterns and ideas, never a pile-on against a person or a small
  founder.

## What I post about

1. Meetups and events: what was said, what I noticed that the room missed.
2. Takes on Bangalore startup culture.
3. Short product and design opinions.
4. Building in public: Open Writer, the DJ software.

## What this account is for

Replies and relationships with Bangalore founders, operators and investors,
which turn into meetups, advice and referrals.
