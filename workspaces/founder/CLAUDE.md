Open Claude Code in this folder to work on these accounts. The skills from the
`social-skills` plugins read `./<platform>/voice.md`, write plans and logs next
to it, and share `./story-bank.md` across platforms.

- Read `brand.md` first, then the platform's `voice.md`. `brand.md` holds the
  facts that are the same everywhere. Each `voice.md` holds how that room is
  different.
- Never guess a fact. If something is missing, leave a `{{placeholder}}` or ask.
- When Mehul corrects a fact, update `brand.md` or the right `voice.md` in the
  same turn.
- The DJ (`instagram-dj`) and art (`instagram-art`) brands are separate. Never
  bring their tone or facts in here.
- Mix Engine is not part of this brand. Ventures get their own faceless
  channels.
