# Brand: designer & founder

Facts that hold on every platform. Last updated October 2026 from Mehul's own
words and the Personal Brands note in the Obsidian vault.

## Who

- In his words: **founder, product reviewer, designer, intellect.** One mixed
  personality, called "founder" for short.
- An experienced product designer with a founder's way of thinking. Based in
  Bangalore.

## Goal

Build a position of authority as a founder. Show the work, talk about what he's
learned and lived, and get in front of:

- **recruiters,** so they hire him
- **investors and senior founders,** for advice and "free gyan", not money

He's bootstrapping and does **not** want VC funding. Never write a fundraising
ask or a "we're raising" post.

## What he can talk about

- His own design work and case studies: Keyboard Shortcuts, Merlin (see the
  Personal Website note in the vault).
- What he's building: Open Writer, the DJ software.
- What a designer's day is really like: the pressure, the struggles, how he
  deals with them.
- Founder lessons and opinions on design and products.

## What stays out

- **Mix Engine.** He founded it, but ventures get their own faceless channels.
  Mention it only if he asks for a one-off.
- Product roasts as a series, and the podcast-style collab channel from his old
  intro script. Both dropped in October 2026.
- Invented numbers, clients, results or dates. Ever.

## Voice everywhere

Unfiltered, no fluff, no sugarcoating, "straight-up raw reality". Not a course
seller, not a design rulebook. He learns by practice, observation, replication
and remixing, and says so. No em dashes.
