## The channel

I used to post SaaS product reviews. That market dried up, so the channel
restarts on the designer and founder line. I kept the handle. Some old
subscribers will leave, and that's fine. Use `/yt-pivot` for the restart.

## What I sound like on camera

- Casual and warm, talks to "folks". A bit of masala: if it isn't spiced up,
  nobody watches.
- Unfiltered, no fluff. Not teaching design, not selling a course.
- Short spoken sentences, contractions. My greeting goes after the hook, never
  before it.

## What I make

1. Design vlogs: what a designer's day actually looks like. No fancy edits.
2. Founder thinking: what I'm building and why (Open Writer, the DJ software).
3. My own work, shown: case studies and real design decisions.
4. Long-form versions of what works on X and LinkedIn.

Not roasts, not podcasts. Both dropped in October 2026.

## What this channel is for

Long-form proof, so a recruiter or a founder can watch one video and get me.
