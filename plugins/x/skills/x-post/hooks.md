# X hook formulas

Eleven shapes for the first line of a tweet or thread. Pick by the goal first,
then by the topic. Fill every slot with a real fact. A shape left unfilled, or
filled with an invented number, is worse than no hook.

| Goal | Reach for |
| --- | --- |
| replies | X3, X1, X9 |
| reposts | X1, X4, X11, X8 |
| likes | X6, X8 |
| bookmarks | X2, X5, X7, X10 |

## Single tweets

**X1 One-liner contrarian** (reposts). One sharp claim you would defend in
person. `Most {group} think {belief}. The ones who {win} do {the opposite}.` Or
the claim alone, flat, no "I think". Fails when it can't survive the replies.

**X2 Data point** (bookmarks). One odd-precision number and one line on why it
matters. `{1.3s / 2.4x / $873} {what it measures}. {why it lands}.` A named
company's specific spend plus a same-week consequence is a strong variant.
Fails the moment the number is wrong.

**X3 Build-in-public confession** (replies). A real number from your own work,
ugly ones included. `{When}: {project} did {metric}. The part nobody tweets:
{the cost or surprise}.` Fails when the vulnerability is manufactured.

**X4 Quote with value** (reposts). Quote someone's take and add the missing
layer: a counter-example, the nuance, the "and here's why". Never a dunk on a
smaller account.

**X5 Mini-list** (bookmarks). `{N} {things} that {payoff}:` then one short line
per item, all inside one tweet. Five real items beat ten padded ones. If items
need explaining, it's X7.

**X6 Relatable cold open** (likes). One line that drops the reader into a
specific shared moment, no setup. The specificity is the whole job. Variants:
the ironic day-in-the-life list (a quoted aspirational premise, then deadpan
bullets that puncture it) and the meme pairing (two things nobody puts
together).

**X11 Third-person case study** (reposts). Someone else's absurd, specific
result, told so the reader thinks they could copy it. `{Person} {did small
thing} and got {outsized result}. Here's the move: {move}.` Prefer it over a
first-person "how I" when the result isn't yours.

## Threads

**X7 Listicle promise** (bookmarks). `{N} {things} that {payoff}. Most people
get {k} of them wrong.` Then one tweet per item that actually teaches it, best
item first or second.

**X8 Story** (likes, reposts). Start at the peak of a true story, raise the
stakes in line 2, then one beat per tweet to the turn and one line of meaning.

**X9 Curiosity gap** (replies, bookmarks). A surprising result stated as fact,
the mechanism held back one tweet. Pay it off by tweet 2.

**X10 How-I teardown** (bookmarks). `How I {result} in {time}:` then one step
per tweet with the real detail (tool, setting, screenshot). A teardown of a
product, screen by screen, is the same shape.

## Micro-rules

- The first line has to work with nothing after it. There's no fold.
- Lowercase sentence starts are native on X. Names stay capitalised.
- One specific number beats any adjective.
- No ALL CAPS openers, no "here's what / here's how" openers, no "The result?".
- No link in the tweet. Put it in a reply.
